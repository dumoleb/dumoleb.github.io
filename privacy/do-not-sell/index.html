Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Your rights under US state privacy laws, and exactly which switch to press.

The short answer: there is nothing to opt out of on our side. Yuki does not sell your personal information, and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. There is no data broker, no audience list, and no third party paying us for anything about you.

What that means precisely

These laws use two specific words, and vagueness helps nobody:

  • Sell - disclosing personal information for money or other valuable consideration. We do not do this. Yuki has no arrangement of any kind under which anyone receives information about you.
  • Share - disclosing personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, meaning adverts targeted at you based on a profile of your activity across different services. We do not do this either. Adverts in Yuki are non-personalised: they are not targeted using a profile of your interests.

What we do transmit, stated plainly

Being accurate matters more than sounding clean, so: Yuki does send two things that these laws care about, even though neither is a sale or a share.

  • Anonymous usage measurement - how the app is used, so we can find problems and decide what to improve. Never your playlists, your channels, your sign-in details, or what you watch.
  • Your device's advertising ID, sent with ad requests. This is a resettable device identifier, not your name or account.

Both can be switched off, and switching them off costs you nothing - the app works identically either way, and no feature sits behind them.

How to opt out

1. In Yuki, on your television

Settings ▸ Protection & history ▸ Share usage data - turn it off. It takes effect immediately, not at the next launch, and it stops the usage measurement described above.

2. In your television's own settings

You can reset or delete the advertising ID itself, which applies across every app on the device, not only Yuki:

  • Google TV: Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads ▸ Delete advertising ID
  • Android TV: Settings ▸ Device Preferences ▸ About ▸ Legal information ▸ Ads

"Delete" opts out entirely - no new identifier is assigned.

3. By writing to us

Send a request to dumoleb@gmail.com and we will act on it. You may also use an authorised agent.

Your other rights

Depending on your state, you may also have the right to know what personal information is collected, to obtain a copy of it, to correct it, to delete it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

Most of these you can exercise yourself, immediately, without asking us. Yuki has no user accounts and runs no servers, so almost everything the app knows about you sits on your own television: clearing the app's data or uninstalling it erases the lot, with no request and no waiting. For anything held by Google on our behalf, write to the address above.

We do not discriminate. Yuki behaves identically whether you opt out or not. There is no reduced tier, no withheld feature, and no different price for viewers who refuse.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16, and we do not sell or share the personal information of minors.

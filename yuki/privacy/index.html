Yuki - Privacy Policy

Effective 16 August 2026 · Yuki for Android TV · com.emberkite.yuki

The short version

Yuki is a media player. You bring your own channel list or media server; Yuki plays it on your television. Yuki has no user accounts and runs no servers of its own, so there is no place for your viewing to be sent to, and there is no profile of you anywhere. Your playlists, credentials, favourites and viewing history stay on your television.

What does leave the device is limited and listed in full below: requests to the provider you configured, purchase checks with Google Play, and Google's analytics and advertising services.

Who is responsible

Yuki is made by dumoleb, an independent developer based in Israel, who is the data controller for this app. Contact: dumoleb@gmail.com.

What stays on your television and is never sent to me

Stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • The addresses and sign-in credentials of the playlists, Xtream portals and media servers you add
  • Your channel lists, programme-guide data and any downloaded artwork
  • Your favourites, watch history, resume positions and watchlist
  • Your profiles, and each profile's settings and customisation
  • Your parental-control PIN and its settings
  • All app settings - language, text size, appearance, and the rest

None of this is transmitted to me, because there is nothing to transmit it to. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data from Android settings, deletes all of it permanently. I cannot recover it, and I cannot see it in the first place.

What leaves the device, and to whom

1. The provider you configured

When you add a playlist, an Xtream portal or a Jellyfin server, Yuki contacts that server directly to fetch your channels, guide data and video - sending the credentials you entered, as any client must. That server is operated by whoever you chose, under their own privacy policy, not mine. I have no relationship with them and no visibility into the exchange.

A note on encryption, stated plainly. Many IPTV providers only offer plain http, so Yuki permits unencrypted connections. Where your provider's address begins http:// rather than https://, that traffic - including your credentials - is not encrypted in transit. This is a property of the provider you chose, not something Yuki can fix on your behalf. Prefer an https provider where one is available.

2. Google Play (purchases)

If you buy Premium, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. I never see your card, billing address or Google account details. Yuki only asks Google Play whether the account on this device currently owns Premium - a yes-or-no answer, nothing more. That is also why Premium follows you to another television signed into the same Google account without any login.

3. Google Firebase (analytics and configuration)

Yuki uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Remote Config, both operated by Google. Firebase automatically collects a standard set of measurements - an app-instance identifier, device model, operating-system version, app version, coarse region and app-open and session events - and Yuki reads a small remote configuration file that controls how advertising behaves.

Beyond Firebase's automatic collection, Yuki itself records exactly one thing: which advertising rollout group your installation belongs to, so I can tell whether showing adverts is harming the experience. Yuki sends no events about what you watch, which channels you have, which provider you use, or what you search for.

4. Advertising

Yuki can show short video adverts, served through Google's Interactive Media Ads. Ad requests include your device's advertising ID and general device information.

  • Adverts are non-personalised. They are not targeted using a profile of your interests.
  • Where your permission is required, no advert is requested until you have given it. You can change your answer at any time in Settings ▸ Protection & history ▸ Share usage data, which stops ad requests as well as measurement.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your television's Google settings.

5. What I do not do

  • No crash or diagnostic reporting service is built into the app.
  • No content, playlist, credential or viewing record is ever uploaded to me or to any third party.
  • I do not sell personal information, and I do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Children

Yuki is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app includes parental controls, which operate entirely on the device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority.

In Yuki's case you can exercise most of these yourself, immediately. Because there is no account and no server-side copy, everything the app knows about you is on your television: clearing the app's data or uninstalling it erases the lot, with no request to me and no waiting.

For the data held by Google on my behalf - Firebase measurements and advertising identifiers - write to dumoleb@gmail.com and I will act on your request. You can also reset your advertising ID yourself in your television's settings, and manage or cancel a subscription directly in Google Play.

Step-by-step instructions, including how to have data held by Google on my behalf deleted, are on the data deletion page.

Security

Your credentials are held in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Release builds of Yuki are obfuscated. Please read the encryption note above: where your provider offers only http, Yuki cannot make that connection private for you.

If this policy changes materially I will update the effective date above and publish the revised version at this address before the change takes effect.

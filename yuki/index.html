Yuki

A player for Android TV and Google TV. You bring your own playlist or media server; Yuki gives it a fast, elegant interface built for a remote control.

Yuki provides no content. It does not host, sell or resell any channels, films, series or subscriptions, and it ships with no content of any kind. To use it you need your own IPTV playlist, your own Xtream account, or your own media server. Yuki is a player, in the same way a music player is not a record label.

What it connects to

  • M3U and M3U8 playlists, by URL
  • Xtream Codes portals
  • Jellyfin media servers
  • Live channels, films and series, with catch-up and replay where your provider offers them

Built for a remote, not a touchscreen

Every screen is laid out for D-pad navigation. Focus is always visible, text is sized to be read across a room, and nothing expects a mouse or a finger. The fullscreen player carries channel lists, a programme guide, audio and subtitle panels and a now-and-next bar, all reachable without leaving what you are watching.

Two playback engines

Some streams play perfectly on one engine and fail on another. Yuki ships both a modern engine and a compatibility engine, and switches automatically when a stream will not start. Awkward playlists that stutter or refuse to load elsewhere often simply work.

Accessibility and languages

Text size, high-visibility focus and menu display time are adjustable. Screens are labelled for screen readers, and layouts mirror properly for right-to-left languages. Yuki ships in English, Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Greek, Turkish and Romanian - all bundled inside the app, so a fresh install is correct in your language before it ever reaches the network.

Privacy

Yuki has no user accounts and runs no servers of its own. Your playlists, credentials, favourites and viewing history stay on your television and are never sent to me. Full detail in the privacy policy.

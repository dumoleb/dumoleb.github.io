Deleting your data

Yuki for Android TV · com.emberkite.yuki

There is no account to delete. Yuki has no user accounts and runs no servers of its own, so there is no profile of you held anywhere for me to erase. Your playlists, credentials, favourites and viewing history are on your television and nowhere else - which means you can delete all of it yourself, immediately, without asking me and without waiting.

Delete everything on your television

Either of these erases the lot, permanently:

  • Uninstall Yuki - Android removes the app's entire private storage with it.
  • Clear the app's data - on your television, open Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Yuki ▸ Clear data. The app stays installed and returns to a fresh state.

That removes all of the following:

  • The addresses and sign-in credentials of every playlist, Xtream portal and media server you added
  • Your channel lists, programme-guide data and downloaded artwork
  • Your favourites, watch history, resume positions and watchlist
  • Your profiles, and each profile's settings and customisation
  • Your parental-control PIN and its settings
  • Every app setting - language, text size, appearance and the rest

This cannot be undone, and I cannot recover any of it for you - I never had a copy. If you only want to remove one profile rather than everything, delete that profile inside the app: its favourites, saved titles and watch history go with it.

Data held by Google on my behalf

Two Google services receive limited data described in full in the privacy policy: Firebase (an app-instance identifier, device model, app version, coarse region, and app-open and session events) and Google's advertising services (your device's advertising ID).

You can act on both yourself, at once:

  • Turn the collection off - in Yuki, open Settings ▸ Protection & history ▸ Share usage data. This stops measurement and advertising requests.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID - in your television's own Google settings.

To have data already held by those services deleted, write to dumoleb@gmail.com from any address, with the subject Data deletion request. Tell me your television's advertising ID if you have it to hand - without some identifier there is nothing for me to look up, because none of it is tied to a name, an email or an account.

I answer as fast as one person can, and in any case within 30 days.

Purchases

Premium purchases are held by Google Play, not by me - I never see your card, billing address or Google account details. Manage or cancel a subscription at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions, and use Google's own tools for anything relating to payment records.

Questions

Write to dumoleb@gmail.com. The full detail of what is collected, by whom, and for how long it is kept is in the privacy policy.

Yuki Setup FAQ Support Privacy

Questions people actually ask

Grouped roughly by when they come up - before installing, while setting up, and once you are using it.

Before you install

Does Yuki come with channels or films?

No, and it never will. Yuki hosts, sells and resells nothing. It is a player: you supply an IPTV playlist you already subscribe to, an Xtream Codes portal, or your own Jellyfin server, and Yuki gives it an interface built for a remote control. A music player ships with no music; this is the same arrangement.

Then what am I paying for, if I buy Premium?

The player, not the content. Your provider charges you for channels; I charge for the software that makes them pleasant to watch on a television. If you have no provider, Premium will not give you one.

Which devices does it run on?

Android TV and Google TV - televisions and streaming boxes. It is deliberately not offered to phones and tablets: every screen is laid out for a D-pad, there is no touch handling anywhere in it, and the type is sized to be read from three metres. It would be a poor phone app, so it is not published as one.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no Yuki account, no email, no password, nothing to create. If you buy Premium it is tied to the Google account already signed in to your television.

Free and Premium

What do I get without paying?

  • Live TV with dual-engine playback
  • Up to two sources (M3U playlist or Xtream portal)
  • Today's programme guide, plus catch-up and replay where your provider offers it
  • Films and series, including resuming something you did not finish
  • Favourites
  • PIN lock and per-channel locks
  • The full interface in twelve languages

That is a usable television app, not a demo. Most people never need more.

What does Premium add?

  • Unlimited sources instead of two
  • The full multi-day programme guide, rather than today's
  • Organising, renaming and grouping your channels
  • Tailoring how the Guide looks
  • Saving films and shows to My List
  • Jellyfin media server support
  • Scheduled locks and screen-time limits

Annual or lifetime?

Both exist. The annual renews each year; the lifetime is one payment that never renews and includes every Premium feature added later. Prices are shown inside the app in your own currency - I do not set them per country by hand, Google converts them.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Google, not by me, and - awkwardly - the Play Store on a television has no subscription screen at all. Cancel from a phone or a computer at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions, signed in with the same Google account. Cancelling keeps Premium running until the period you have already paid for ends.

I paid, and Premium is not on

Yuki re-checks with Google Play periodically rather than continuously, so a purchase made moments ago can take a little while to appear - and the same is true in reverse after a refund. Force-stopping and reopening the app usually will not speed this up, because it is a timed re-check rather than one that happens on launch.

If it still has not appeared after a few hours, write to me with the Google account you bought it on and I will look.

Will Premium work on my second television?

Yes, with nothing to do. Sign the other television in to the same Google account and Premium is already on the first time you open Yuki. There is no restore button because there is nothing to restore - it is resolved from your Google account automatically.

Setting up

My provider gave me a link and a username - which do I choose?

Choose Xtream Codes. It gives Yuki a proper programme guide, films and series as separate libraries, and catch-up where offered. A plain playlist link can only describe a list of channels. The setup guide walks through both.

It says it cannot connect, and I am sure the details are right

Retype the server address by hand before anything else. A trailing slash, a copied space, or a missing port number account for most of these, and none of them are visible when you look at the field.

Why is the first load so slow?

Because it is downloading your entire channel list and, where your provider supplies it, several days of programme listings for potentially thousands of channels. It happens once; afterwards Yuki refreshes quietly in the background.

Some channels do not play, but others do

That is almost always the stream itself rather than the app. Yuki already tries a second, more tolerant playback engine automatically when the first one fails, which rescues many awkward streams - but a stream that is offline, geo-blocked or simply broken at the provider cannot be fixed from here. If the same channels fail for you in another player, it is the provider.

Where is catch-up? I cannot find it in the menu

It lives in the Guide, not in a menu of its own: move back through the programme grid to something that has already aired and open it. A dedicated catch-up screen is not part of this release.

Living with it

Can everyone in the house have their own setup?

Yes. Each profile keeps its own favourites, viewing history and resume positions, and its own preferences like text size and row order. What belongs to the television rather than the person - your connected sources, the parental PIN, the app's language - is shared, which is usually what you want.

I forgot my parental PIN

I cannot recover it. The PIN never leaves your television and I have no copy of it, which is precisely what makes it worth having. Clearing the app's data from Android settings resets it, at the cost of your sources and settings.

Is the text too small from where I sit?

Settings has a text-size control that applies across the whole app rather than to one screen, along with a high-visibility focus option and a control for how long menus stay on screen. They are worth a minute if you sit further back than usual.

Which languages are there?

Twelve: English, Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Greek, Turkish and Polish. Hebrew and Arabic mirror the entire interface, not only the text. All twelve ship inside the app, so switching works offline and needs no download.

What do you know about what I watch?

Nothing. There is no Yuki server for it to go to. Your playlists, credentials, favourites and viewing history stay on your television. What does leave the device is listed in full in the privacy policy - and the app sends no event about what you watch, which channels you have, or which provider you use.

Something is wrong and none of this covers it

Try the troubleshooting page - it works through the app's actual error messages one by one, so you can search it for the exact words on your screen. If that does not cover it, write to dumoleb@gmail.com; the support page lists the handful of details that turn a week of guessing into a same-day fix.

Never send your playlist URL, portal password or server login - to me or to anyone. I have no access to your provider and cannot use them to help you, and they are everything somebody would need to take over your subscription.

Troubleshooting

Yuki's messages are written to say what actually went wrong rather than "an error occurred". This page explains what each one means and what usually fixes it.

One idea worth holding on to. Yuki talks to a server that belongs to someone else - your IPTV provider, or your own Jellyfin machine. A large share of problems are that server saying no, and no setting in the app can overrule it. The messages below try to tell you honestly which side of that line you are on.

Adding a playlist

"Couldn't reach this URL. Check your internet and the address."

Nothing answered at all. Either the television is offline, or the address is wrong in a way that means nothing is listening - a typo in the host, or a missing or wrong port number. Open something else on the television to prove the connection, then retype the address by hand rather than trusting a paste.

"The server responded with an error (HTTP 401 / 403). Check the URL."

The server answered, and refused. Those two codes almost always mean the subscription has expired, the credentials in the link are wrong, or the provider has capped how many devices can use it at once. Yuki is being told no by name - only your provider can change that answer.

"The server responded with an error (HTTP 404)."

The server is there but the path is not. Usually a truncated link - long playlist URLs are easy to cut short when copying.

"The playlist took too long to download. Try again or check the URL."

A timeout, not a refusal. Very large playlists on a slow connection genuinely can exceed it, and so can a provider under load. Trying again a few minutes later is a reasonable first move; if it never completes, the provider is the place to ask.

The import finished but there are far fewer channels than expected

Then the playlist itself is what your provider served. Yuki imports what it is given and does not filter anything out. If the same link produces more channels elsewhere, send me the details from the support page and I will look - but check first that you are not looking at a filtered view, such as a single group or your favourites.

Watching

"This channel's stream is not available. Please contact your content provider."

This message means something specific: Yuki tried every way it has of playing that stream, including the second, more tolerant playback engine, and all of them failed. It is not a first complaint - it is what is left after the automatic retries are exhausted.

In practice that is a stream which is off the air, geo-blocked, or broken at the provider. The wording is deliberate: at this point the provider really is the only party who can fix it.

One channel fails and the rest are fine

Almost always that single stream, not the app or your connection - the others are proof the connection works. Worth trying at a different time of day: providers frequently have channels that only fail under load.

Everything stutters or buffers

Buffering means the video is arriving more slowly than it is being watched. In order of how often it turns out to be the cause:

  • The provider is overloaded - typically evenings and live sport. Same channel, better at 3pm, is the tell.
  • Wi-Fi - televisions are often the worst-placed device in the house. If you can test with a cable, that answers it in one step.
  • The stream's own bitrate - a 4K feed on a connection that cannot sustain it will never settle, no matter how long it buffers.

Yuki cannot make a stream arrive faster than your provider sends it. What it does do is switch playback engine automatically when a stream will not start at all, which is a different problem from one that starts and stutters.

"This stream can't be moved to a different position."

You tried to skip within something the server will not let you seek in. Live streams usually cannot be scrubbed, and some films and series are served in a form that does not support it either. Playback itself is unaffected.

The programme guide

There is no guide data, or only the current programme

Guide data comes from your provider, not from Yuki, and providers vary enormously: some send a fortnight, some send today, some send nothing at all. An Xtream Codes portal generally carries a much better guide than a plain M3U playlist - if you have both kinds of access, that alone is a reason to prefer the portal.

"Catchup not available for this program" / "no longer available for catchup"

Two different answers. The first means that programme was never offered for replay; the second means it was, and the window has closed. How far back catch-up reaches is your provider's decision and varies channel by channel on the same subscription.

Catch-up is reached through the Guide - move back to a programme that has already aired and open it. There is no separate catch-up screen in this release.

"Starts later - preview not available"

You have selected a programme that has not begun. Nothing is wrong; there is simply nothing to show yet.

Jellyfin

"Can't reach your Jellyfin server right now. Check it's on and connected."

Nothing answered. Confirm the machine is awake - servers that sleep are a common cause - and that you are using an address the television can actually reach. A local address only works while the television is on the same network.

"Sign-in was incorrect. Check your details and try again."

The server answered and rejected the credentials. Both fields are case-sensitive.

"This account can't access any libraries on the server."

Sign-in succeeded, but that Jellyfin user has not been granted any libraries. Fix it in Jellyfin's own user settings - it is a permission on the server, not a Yuki setting.

Sources and refreshing

A source shows "failed" after a refresh

Your source is still configured - that refresh attempt failed, which is not the same as the source being broken. The app says so in as many words: "Your sources are still set up - this read just failed." Refreshes retry on their own, and most transient failures resolve without you doing anything.

I changed my playlist and the old channels are still there

Yuki keeps what it has until a refresh succeeds - deleting your channel list because one download failed would be much worse behaviour. Refresh the source from Settings to pull the new list immediately.

Purchases

I bought Premium and it has not unlocked

Yuki re-checks with Google Play on a timer rather than on every launch, so a very recent purchase can take a while to appear - and force-stopping the app will not hurry it along, because the check is not triggered by opening the app. If it has still not appeared after several hours, write to me.

I cannot find where to cancel

Not your fault: the Play Store on a television has no subscription screen at all. Use a phone or a computer, at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions, signed in with the same Google account.

When it is worth writing to me

If the answer above points at your provider, asking them first will nearly always be faster. If it points at the app - or nothing here fits - the support page lists the few details that let me reproduce a problem instead of guessing at it.

Never send your playlist URL, portal password or server login - to me or to anyone. I have no access to your provider and cannot use them to help you, and they are everything somebody would need to take over your subscription.

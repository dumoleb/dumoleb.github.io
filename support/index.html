Support

Write to dumoleb@gmail.com. I read everything, and I answer as fast as one person can.

Reporting a problem with Yuki

These few details usually turn a week of guessing into a same-day fix:

  • Your television or streaming box, and its Android version
  • The Yuki version, from Settings
  • What kind of source you use - M3U playlist, Xtream portal, or Jellyfin
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • Whether it affects one channel or title, or all of them
Please never send me your playlist URL, portal password or server login. I have no access to your provider, so they would tell me nothing I could act on - and I would rather not hold them. What helps instead is what you saw: the message on screen, how long it waited before failing, and whether it happened once or every time.

Things I cannot help with

Yuki is a player. It provides no channels, films or subscriptions of its own, so I cannot help you find, buy or fix a source. If your channels have stopped working, are missing, or were never there, that is a question for whoever provides your playlist or portal - I have no visibility into it and no relationship with them.

Requesting a feature

Tell me the problem rather than the solution - what you were trying to do when the app got in your way. That is far more useful than a feature name, and it often turns out there is a better fix than the one either of us first imagined.

Billing, refunds and subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play, not by me - I never see your card or billing details. Manage or cancel a subscription in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. Refunds are requested through Google Play. If something you paid for is not working, write to me and I will help sort it out.

Privacy and your data

See the privacy policy. Most of what Yuki knows sits on your own television, so clearing the app's data or uninstalling it erases the lot without asking me. For anything else, write to dumoleb@gmail.com.
